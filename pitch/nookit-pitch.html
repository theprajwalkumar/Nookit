The problem

Everyday Mac work is death
by a thousand apps.

01

Fragmentation

Convert an image, compress a PDF, rename a batch, trim a clip: four tasks, four apps, four interfaces, four learning curves.

02

Subscription fatigue

Each utility wants $5–$15 a month for a feature used once a week. The bills add up faster than the value does.

03

Privacy risk

Free online converters are the default, and they ask users to upload contracts, IDs and client media to someone else's server.

04

Dead space

The notch sits unused while windows, menu bar icons and Dock tiles multiply around it.

Users pay three times — in money, in time, and in trust.

Why now

Four shifts make this
the right moment.

01

On-device AI is useful

Apple Intelligence and local models made private assistance practical. Nookit can offer AI without shipping prompts to a vendor.

02

Subscriptions are unpopular

Users actively look for one-time alternatives to the monthly bundle. “Buy it once” is a feature again.

03

Privacy is a buying criterion

Uploading client files to a free converter is no longer acceptable in studios, clinics, legal and finance work.

04

Apple silicon is fast enough

Media encoding, PDF rendering and image work that once needed a server now finish on the laptop, instantly.

Nookit · Why now06
The customer

People whose job is
handling other people's files.

01

Primary: the Mac power user

Freelancers, designers, developers, consultants and students who use a MacBook as their main machine and touch media, PDFs and files every day. They already pay for four to seven single-purpose apps and resent it.

02

Secondary: small teams and education

Studios, agencies, clinics and schools of 2–20 people that want a standard local utility set without per-seat SaaS billing or a data-processing agreement.

Where they already are
  • Mac communities — Reddit, MacRumors, Hacker News, designer and developer Slacks.
  • Productivity newsletters — the same channels that sell one-time Mac utilities today.
  • Search — 94 tool pages that answer “how do I merge a PDF on Mac” without an upload.
  • Creator reviews — Mac YouTube and podcast coverage of indie utilities.

The wedge: one person can replace four apps and one subscription in a single $29 purchase.

Nookit · Competition09
Go to market

Start where Macs are,
then follow the language.

Primary · United States

Largest Mac installed base, highest willingness to pay for one-time utilities, strongest newsletter and creator reach.

Secondary · UK, Canada, Australia

English-speaking Mac markets where the storefront, USD pricing and support already work without translation.

Third wave · Western Europe + Japan

Strong Mac share and paid-software culture; needs localised pricing and checkout, not a translated app.

Long term · India

Price-sensitive but strong developer and student audiences: purchasing-power pricing and an education licence lead here.

Channels already built
  • 108 prerendered routes, including 94 tool pages, already indexed and answer-first.
  • Search intent — “merge PDF on Mac”, “HEIC to PNG”, “JWT decoder” with no upload step.

  • Creator reviews — hands-on Mac YouTube and podcasts.
